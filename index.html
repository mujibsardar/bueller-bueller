Seat Maps & Student Names

A classroom tool for seats and names.

Bueller, Bueller is an iPhone app for teachers. It keeps seating charts and name practice on the device. There is no account.

Seat Map

Photograph the classroom, place desks, and assign students. Zoom from the whole room to names and photos. Switch periods and charts. Print or share a PDF.

Learn Names

Practice one class at a time on Easy, Medium, or Hard. Continue a saved quiz and track mastery. A seating chart is not required.

On this iPhone

Student names, photos, charts, and progress stay in the app's private storage. A passcode and a cover hide the class when you leave the app. The download is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription inside the app.
