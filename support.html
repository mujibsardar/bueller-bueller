Support

Bueller, Bueller is an offline iPhone app for teachers. Seat Map is for classroom charts. Learn Names is for practicing a class roster. Both work without an account.

Where records live

Student names, photos, seating charts, quiz progress, and game progress stay on the iPhone. The developer does not receive a copy. See the Privacy Policy.

Passcode

The first launch asks for a 4- or 6-digit passcode. Leaving the app covers the screen. When a passcode is saved, opening the app again asks for it. Change it in Settings → Passcode. The app cannot show the original digits back to you, because only a hash is stored in the iPhone Keychain.

Delete data

  • To remove one student, edit that student and choose Delete Student. The saved photo and name-quiz progress for that student are removed. The seat is left empty.
  • To remove a chart, delete it from the chart screen. Its classroom photo and seating-game progress for that chart are removed.
  • To remove the app’s local files, delete the app from the iPhone. The passcode hash is in iPhone Keychain and may remain after the app is deleted.

Printing and sharing

Export builds a PDF from the options on screen and hands it to iOS. You choose where a share goes. Printing uses the printer you select. Those destinations are not operated by this app.
